FAQ

Questions clients ask

How much does a website cost?

A website costs Rs 12,000. A website with a complete SEO setup costs Rs 20,000. Graphic design starts from Rs 1,200 per design, and the full price list is in the pricing section. I confirm the total before we start.

What kind of websites do you build?

Business, institutional and e-commerce websites, hand-built rather than assembled in a page builder, in English, Urdu, Arabic, or all three on one domain. Technical SEO is part of every build.

Do you handle right-to-left languages properly?

Yes. Urdu and Arabic sites get true right-to-left layout, mirrored components and proper typefaces (Nastaliq and Naskh), plus hreflang so search engines serve the right language to the right visitor.

How do we get started?

Send the business name, the languages it needs and roughly when you want it live, on WhatsApp or by email. I reply with what it takes and what it costs. No call is required.

Do you take care of the domain and hosting?

Yes. I set up the domain, hosting, SSL and Search Console, and stay with the site through the first weeks of indexing, so it is live and being found, not just handed over.

Can you guarantee Google rankings?

No one honestly can. What I do is build the technical foundation, research keywords in your real market, and measure results in Search Console so you can see what is working.

Which markets do you work in?

Pakistan, the UAE and Gulf, the UK and the United States.
